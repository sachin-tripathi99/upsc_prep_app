## Getting Started

### Installation and Setup

1. **Clone and Install**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/sachin-tripathi99/upsc_prep_app.git
   cd upsc_prep_app
   npm install
   cd client && npm install && cd ..
   ```

2. **Start the Application**:
   ```bash
   # Run both frontend and backend
   npm run dev
   ```

3. **Access the App**:
   - Open your browser and navigate to: `http://localhost:3000`
   - The backend API runs on: `http://localhost:5000`

## Features Guide

### 1. Home Page
The home page provides an overview of your preparation journey:
- **Today's Study Plan**: See your daily tasks based on the roadmap
- **Progress Statistics**: Quick view of your achievements
- **Feature Links**: Easy navigation to all sections

### 2. Daily News Updates

**Purpose**: Stay updated with current affairs from The Hindu newspaper

**How to Use**:
1. Navigate to "Daily News" from the menu
2. Browse articles categorized by subject (Economy, Polity, etc.)
3. Filter by category and importance level
4. Click "Read More" to view full article with analysis
5. Click "Mark as Read" to track your progress
6. Note the tags and related topics for comprehensive understanding

**Tips**:
- Read at least 5-10 articles daily
- Focus on HIGH importance articles first
- Make notes of key points
- Connect news with static topics (Polity, Economy, etc.)
- Revise weekly compilations

### 3. Smart Flashcards

**Purpose**: Effective revision using spaced repetition algorithm

**How to Use**:
1. Navigate to "Flashcards" from the menu
2. Select category, difficulty, or use random mode
3. Read the question carefully
4. Try to answer mentally before revealing
5. Click "Show Answer" to see the correct answer
6. Rate your recall:
   - **Didn't Know** (😫): Will appear very soon
   - **Partially** (🤔): Will appear moderately soon
   - **Knew Well** (😄): Will appear after longer interval

**Study Modes**:
- **Sequential**: Study cards in order by category
- **Random**: Mix up cards for varied practice

**Tips**:
- Review cards daily for 30-60 minutes
- Be honest with your ratings
- Create your own flashcards for weak topics
- Focus on cards you rated "Didn't Know"
- Review before exams for quick revision

### 4. 15-Month Roadmap

**Purpose**: Comprehensive day-by-day preparation plan

**How to Use**:

**Overview Section**:
1. Click "15-Month Roadmap" from menu
2. Review the 4 preparation phases
3. Understand daily routine recommendations
4. Read success tips carefully

**Monthly View**:
1. Click "View Monthly Plan"
2. Select month from the tabs
3. See weekly focus areas
4. Click on any day card to see detailed tasks

**Daily View**:
1. Select a specific day
2. Review all tasks for that day
3. Study according to the plan
4. Mark day as complete when done
5. Track your progress

**Features**:
- **450 Days** of detailed planning
- **Month 1-2** fully detailed with specific tasks
- **Study Hours**: Recommended time for each day
- **Topics**: Focus areas for the day
- **Tasks**: Specific activities to complete

**Tips**:
- Follow the roadmap strictly
- Don't skip days - consistency is key
- Complete tasks before marking as done
- Adjust study hours based on your capacity
- Revise previous topics regularly

### 5. Mock Tests

**Purpose**: Practice and assess your preparation level

**How to Use**:
1. Navigate to "Mock Tests"
2. Browse available tests
3. Read test details (duration, questions, marks)
4. Click "Start Test" when ready
5. Complete the test within time limit
6. View your results and performance

**Available Tests**:
- Prelims GS Paper 1 (100 questions, 200 marks, 120 minutes)
- CSAT Mock Tests (80 questions, 200 marks, 120 minutes)

**Tips**:
- Start taking tests from Month 6
- Simulate exam conditions
- Attempt easier questions first
- Manage time effectively
- Analyze incorrect answers
- Identify weak areas for revision
- Take at least 20-30 full-length tests before exam

### 6. Progress Dashboard

**Purpose**: Monitor your preparation progress

**Features**:
1. **Study Streak**: Consecutive days of study
2. **Completion Rate**: Percentage of roadmap completed
3. **Articles Read**: Number of news articles consumed
4. **Flashcards Reviewed**: Total revision count
5. **Mock Tests Taken**: Practice test attempts
6. **Study Time**: Total hours invested

**Milestones**:
- 7-Day Streak
- 30 Days Completed
- 100 News Articles
- 500 Flashcards Reviewed
- 10 Mock Tests
- 30-Day Streak

**Tips**:
- Check progress weekly
- Maintain study streak for consistency
- Celebrate milestone achievements
- Identify areas needing more focus
- Adjust study plan based on analytics

## Study Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation Building (Months 1-4)
**Focus**: NCERTs and basic concepts
- Complete all NCERT books (Class 6-12)
- Build strong foundation in all subjects
- Start current affairs reading
- Make comprehensive notes
- Practice 50-100 MCQs weekly

### Phase 2: Advanced Preparation (Months 5-9)
**Focus**: Standard books and answer writing
- Read standard reference books
- Daily current affairs compilation
- Answer writing practice (2-3 questions daily)
- Previous year paper analysis
- Take monthly mock tests

### Phase 3: Revision & Practice (Months 10-13)
**Focus**: Intensive revision and mock tests
- Revise all subjects (3rd revision)
- Daily flashcard practice
- Weekly full-length mock tests
- Current affairs consolidation
- Improve answer writing speed

### Phase 4: Final Preparation (Months 14-15)
**Focus**: Last-minute revision and confidence
- Quick revision of important topics
- Daily mock tests
- Current affairs final compilation
- Mental preparation
- Health and stress management

### Daily Study Schedule

**Morning Session (6 AM - 12 PM)**: 6 hours
- Core subject reading
- Concept building
- Note making

**Afternoon Session (12 PM - 2 PM)**: 2 hours
- Current affairs (The Hindu + PIB)
- News analysis

**Evening Session (4 PM - 8 PM)**: 4 hours
- Revision
- Flashcard practice
- Previous topics review

**Night Session (8 PM - 10 PM)**: 2 hours
- Answer writing practice
- MCQ solving
- Daily planning

**Total**: 8-10 hours of focused study

## Tips for Success

### General Tips
1. **Consistency Over Intensity**: Study regularly rather than in bursts
2. **Quality Notes**: Make concise, revision-friendly notes
3. **Active Learning**: Don't just read, analyze and connect
4. **Regular Revision**: Revise each topic at least 3 times
5. **Time Management**: Stick to schedule, avoid distractions

### Subject-Specific Tips

**History**:
- Prepare timeline charts
- Connect events causally
- Focus on major movements and personalities
- Relate with current issues

**Polity**:
- Read Constitution and bare acts
- Understand amendments
- Follow Parliament proceedings
- Connect with current affairs

**Geography**:
- Make maps and diagrams
- Understand rather than memorize
- Link physical and human geography
- Focus on India-specific topics

**Economy**:
- Understand concepts clearly
- Follow Economic Survey and Budget
- Read financial newspapers
- Connect theory with current data

**Environment**:
- Focus on government initiatives
- Understand international conventions
- Track environmental news
- Link with sustainable development

**Current Affairs**:
- Read The Hindu daily (mandatory)
- Follow PIB and Yojana magazine
- Make monthly compilations
- Connect with static topics
- Focus on government schemes and policies

### Mental and Physical Health
1. **Exercise Daily**: 30-60 minutes
2. **Meditate**: 15-20 minutes for focus
3. **Sleep Well**: 6-7 hours minimum
4. **Eat Healthy**: Balanced diet
5. **Take Breaks**: Short breaks every 90 minutes
6. **Stay Positive**: Believe in yourself
7. **Social Support**: Stay connected with family

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. ❌ Skipping current affairs
2. ❌ Not making notes
3. ❌ Ignoring previous year papers
4. ❌ Starting answer writing late
5. ❌ Not taking mock tests seriously
6. ❌ Studying without breaks
7. ❌ Comparing with others
8. ❌ Neglecting health
9. ❌ Not revising regularly
10. ❌ Giving up too soon
